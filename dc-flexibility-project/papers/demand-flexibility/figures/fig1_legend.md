**Fig. 1 | Ample fleet headroom, small value relative to compute cost.**
**a,** Energy-system cost savings from task flexibility for GPU-heavy LLM training at the planning-stage economic optimum, relative to the corresponding energy system without flexibility. Each valid representative 0.5° grid supplies the value for its 2° block, clipped to Natural Earth land boundaries and displayed in Robinson projection. Colours span 0–40%; grey land has no displayed value. Of 4,842 valid grids, 4,832 have non-empty land intersections; all 4,842 enter the unweighted continental and global statistics. Bars show continental medians.
**b,** Mean task power and remaining headroom as percentages of each scenario's pre-scheduling task peak, K, across its native observation window. Numbers within the pale segments indicate headroom (%). K excludes idle IT power and cooling; the energy model allows no added task capacity. Colours identify use, and lighter to darker shades distinguish CPU-heavy, balanced and GPU-heavy specifications.
**c,** Fleet annualized cost (squares), median energy-system cost without flexibility (diamonds), and median flexibility savings at planning (filled circles) and as-built (open circles) boundaries, for 10 MW reference fleets. The horizontal axis is logarithmic. Energy costs and savings are converted to equivalent annual amounts using 8,760 divided by native-window hours. Fleet costs use whole-server prices and equivalent server counts, annualized at 0.3319 (four years, 8% discount rate, 3% annual maintenance).
**d,** Savings relative to annualized fleet cost across 4,842 grids per scenario and boundary. Boxes span the 25th–75th percentiles, centre lines mark medians, and whiskers show the full minimum–maximum range. Filled and open boxes indicate planning and as-built boundaries. The dashed line marks 3%; the maximum is 1.87%. All energy comparisons use paired optimal solutions with no imposed local carbon-free matching target. Annual equivalents from the 120-hour cloud window have limited temporal representativeness. Source data are provided.
